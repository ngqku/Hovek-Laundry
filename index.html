Our Core Promises

The Hovek Quality Standard

Every garment that enters our facility is inspected, tracked, and treated with artisan attention to detail.

Fabric Safety Guarantee

We inspect every zipper, button, and seam. If any fabric is ever damaged or compromised, our comprehensive coverage will replace or repair it immediately.

Zero Toxic Solvents

We strictly ban carcinogenic PERC solvent and optical brighteners. Our closed-loop wet-cleaning process preserves fibers and protects local waterways.

Punctual 24h Turnaround

We value your time. We stick to guaranteed 60-minute pickup and delivery windows, so you never have to wait around all day.

Sanitized Packaging

Garments are sealed in dust-proof packaging to ensure they remain pristine from our presses to your closet.
